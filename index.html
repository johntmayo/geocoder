Mapbox Batch Geocoder

Instructions:
1. Upload your CSV file with address columns and City, State (Zip optional)
   Option A: Address, City, State, Zip
   Option B: House, Street, City, State, Zip
2. Click "Geocode Addresses"
3. Download your results with Latitude/Longitude filled in

Drag and drop a CSV here, or choose one from your computer.

CSV should have either: Address, City, State — or — House, Street, City, State (Zip, Latitude, Longitude, Resident Name are optional)
If pasted text has recognized headers, it loads as CSV. Otherwise, each line is treated as a full Address.
